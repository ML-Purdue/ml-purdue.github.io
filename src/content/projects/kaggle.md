## Overview

Work on Kaggle competitions together, then transition to our own
intermediate-level project.

**Difficulty: Beginner.**

## Training

Members will need:

1. Kaggle
2. Coding — some prior experience expected
3. ML/AI — we go through this together

## Milestones

**October 1 — Finish one Kaggle competition.** Introduce team members to ML/AI,
have them work through courses, start a project, and finish it.

**November 1 — Finish another Kaggle competition.** Choose one with a harder
technology, read research, start the project, and finish it.

**December 1 — Transition into our own project.** Reach out by email and message
people for help, agree to join, then start working to solve their problem, or
look for problems to solve.

## Team split

The team has 4–6 members. Initially there are no subteams, but as we become more
independent we split into subteams, each focused on a certain part of the model
and reading relevant papers. The goal is to start involved and give members the
skills to work by themselves soon after.

Meetings occur every one to two weeks.
