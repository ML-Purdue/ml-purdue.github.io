TE AI Cup is an annual international collegiate competition with over 40
universities competing. Teams work with professionals from TE Connectivity to
fix a real-world issue and have an impact on their business, with the
opportunity to patent the team's work. **This is a year-long commitment, with a
presentation to TE's executives in May.**

ML@Purdue has competed every year since 2021, with a history of excellence in
this competition — annually reaching the top-3 podium and receiving large
scholarship and monetary prizes. Because a new team enters each cycle, this page
covers the program as a whole rather than any single year.

## Typical project tracks

The topic changes every year, but projects generally fall into one of three
areas.

**AI Machine Vision (AIMV)** — AI sorting, defect inspection, defect location,
dimensional measurement, vision guidance, 3D vision.

**AI Process Control & Optimization** — manufacturing process variation
minimization, anomaly detection and prevention for manufacturing processes, EHS,
power consumption monitoring and control, AI-based process control and
optimization.

**General AI Applications** — AI for product design, business execution,
planning, demand forecasting, inventory control, tooling management, quality
improvement, SAP, logistics, and marketing.
