Captain (YC F25) is building an AI-native indexing and querying platform for
large-scale unstructured data. Alongside ML@Purdue and Purdue Ventures
Consulting, we're building [DeepQuery.org](https://deepquery.org) — an
open-source, unbiased benchmarking platform for enterprise AI search tools.

DeepQuery.org is an interactive, research-grade platform for evaluating
enterprise retrieval systems, inspired by tools like
[LMArena](https://lmarena.ai/).

## Goals

- Implement a **benchmarking pipeline** supporting multiple retrieval
  architectures (Captain, RAG, GraphRAG, Elasticsearch, and others)
- Host small, medium, and large **datasets** under a tier system to measure
  speed, accuracy, and cost
- Display results in a **public arena-style interface** where users compare tool
  outputs side by side
- Publish a **research paper** covering methodology, results, and use-case
  recommendations

This work serves the broader AI community by clarifying when to use different
retrieval approaches, reducing vendor hype, and guiding enterprise adoption.

Project members gain industry experience, research experience, inside access to
Captain, and a close view of startup life.
